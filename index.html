Regional footprint

Where each builder actually operates

state-level, read from each 10-K

Builders do not share a common map. Horton's "Southeast" and Toll's "South" cover different states entirely, so the segment names on a filing mean nothing without the underlying states behind them. +6 to +18 mo

Land and permit pipeline

The layer closest to your own work. Authorized-but-not-started backlog is the sharpest series here: when builders hold paper and decline to break ground, starts fall about two quarters later.

+1 to +3 mo

Demand and credit

Watch the gap between the 30-year mortgage rate and the 10-year Treasury separately from the rate itself. That spread has run historically wide since 2022, and compression alone would improve affordability with no Fed move at all.

+3 to +12 mo

Capital markets

the rate complex and credit spreads

USD LIBOR stopped publishing in June 2023, so it is deliberately absent. SOFR replaced it, and the 30-day and 90-day averages here are the free public stand-ins for 1-month and 3-month Term SOFR, which CME licenses and FRED cannot carry. Prime is what smaller land and construction facilities actually price off. The 2s10s curve carries the longest lead of anything on this sheet.

Coincident

Activity now

These confirm rather than predict. Their value is calibration: if the leading layer turned three months ago and this layer has not followed, either the signal was noise or the lag is running long.

Inputs

Cost and labor

Materials cost reaches pro formas before it reaches completed cost. Construction job openings are the better labor read than headline employment, because hiring intent turns before payrolls do.

Resale market

Existing homes, three levels

National, California, Ventura County

Existing-home sales, not new construction, which the rest of this board already covers through the builders themselves. All three levels come from one consistent source (Redfin's Data Center) rather than three different ones, so the comparison across geography is actually apples to apples. Neither FRED nor SEC EDGAR carry closed-sale price or volume below the national level.

Equity

Share price

unofficial source, not guaranteed

Pulled from an unofficial Yahoo Finance endpoint, since neither FRED nor SEC EDGAR carry equity prices. This is the least stable source on the board and can fail or lag without notice; a gap here shows up as a missing row, not a wrong number. Year-to-date is measured from each stock's first close of the current calendar year. The two delisted builders show their final trading price rather than a live quote, since neither trades anymore.
