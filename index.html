Lead-time spine

Every series on this sheet sits somewhere on a timeline. Permit backlog and builder land decisions see furthest ahead; delinquency and completions only confirm what already happened. Position on this axis is the reason to watch a series, not its headline number.

These are the agencies' filed release dates, not estimates. The sheet reads them each morning, so a shifted or cancelled release shows up here without anyone adjusting anything.

Builder brief

What the public builders are saying

Rewritten whenever a builder files a new 10-Q. Lot counts, incentive load and regional commentary sit in MD&A prose rather than tagged data, so this section is read out of the filings themselves rather than scraped.

+6 to +18 mo

Land and permit pipeline

The layer closest to your own work. Authorized-but-not-started backlog is the sharpest series here: when builders hold paper and decline to break ground, starts fall about two quarters later.

+1 to +3 mo

Demand and credit

Watch the gap between the 30-year mortgage rate and the 10-year Treasury separately from the rate itself. That spread has run historically wide since 2022, and compression alone would improve affordability with no Fed move at all.

+3 to +12 mo

Capital markets

the rate complex and credit spreads

USD LIBOR stopped publishing in June 2023, so it is deliberately absent. SOFR replaced it, and the 30-day and 90-day averages here are the free public stand-ins for 1-month and 3-month Term SOFR, which CME licenses and FRED cannot carry. Prime is what smaller land and construction facilities actually price off. The 2s10s curve carries the longest lead of anything on this sheet.

Coincident

Activity now

These confirm rather than predict. Their value is calibration: if the leading layer turned three months ago and this layer has not followed, either the signal was noise or the lag is running long.

Inputs

Cost and labor

Materials cost reaches pro formas before it reaches completed cost. Construction job openings are the better labor read than headline employment, because hiring intent turns before payrolls do.

−6 to −12 mo

Distress

Lagging by construction, but useful for confirming that a turn was real rather than a two-month wobble.

State-level series that move entitlement throughput before they move price, and that most people watching national housing data never look at.

Latest filing and real estate inventory carried on the balance sheet, pulled from each company's XBRL data the day it files. Lot counts owned versus optioned are disclosed in prose rather than tagged data, so those are not here. Ask for them when a quarter lands and they get read by hand.

Prediction markets

thin liquidity, read with caution

Kept small on purpose. Total housing volume across these venues runs under a million dollars, so a few traders can move an implied probability several points. A crowd mood ring, not a forecast.
